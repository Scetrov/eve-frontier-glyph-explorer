02 / PRE-ERA DEVELOPMENT

Phase context

“Pre-era” refers to the early development phases before Founders Access and the Era model, rather than to a continuously operating game world.

PHASE 1 / 06 DEC 2022

Out in the open

The project was first introduced to the world, marking the beginning of development out in the open.

PHASE 2 / 06 DEC 2023

Iterative testing

Early systems continued through iterative testing as the project progressed toward technical stability.

PHASE 3 / 21 MAY 2024

Core-systems evaluation

A preliminary testing stage used to evaluate core game systems before major architectural overhauls.

PHASE 4 / 27 SEP—07 NOV 2024

Technical milestone

Python 3.12 and a cloud-native server arrived. After initial hourly crashes, a relaunch improved stability to more than three weeks of continuous uptime.

PHASE 5 / 07 NOV—10 DEC 2024

Before the Era model

The final testing stage before the transition to the persistent, always-on Era model and Founders Access.

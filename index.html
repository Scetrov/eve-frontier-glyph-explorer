04 / PROVENANCE

Method and data

01

Carrier separated

The large diamond and black centre holes are treated as registration graphics. The orange-ring state carries the 9×9 payload.

02

Codebook reconciled

Canonical patterns come from ArchiveInvest. Local Cycle 4–6 captures are retained separately and marked provisional where automatically classified.

03

Context preserved

Every occurrence retains its recording, ordinal position, timestamp, neighbours, and classification provenance.
